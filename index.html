    • On the Biology of a Large Language ModelJack Lindsey et al.Paper · 2025

      Anthropic uses circuit tracing to investigate selected internal mechanisms in Claude 3.5 Haiku. Attribution graphs connect interpretable features into hypotheses about how the model produces an answer; perturbation experiments then test those hypotheses.

      Anthropic / Transformer Circuits

      • Case studies examine multi-step reasoning, planning in poems, multilingual representations, hallucinations and the faithfulness of written reasoning.
      • The analysis uses an approximate replacement model. Its graphs explain only part of the computation, and the selected examples do not establish how the model behaves in every context.

      This piece first piqued my interest in learning about AI architecture.

      Original link Read the original paper

    • Analogy as the Core of CognitionDouglas HofstadterVideo · 2006

      In this Stanford Presidential Lecture, Douglas Hofstadter examines analogy as a central process in thought: how people categorize situations and connect them through shared structure.

      Stanford

      • The Stanford event archive describes the lecture as an examination of mental categories and the connections between them.
      • The lecture took place in February 2006; the Stanford video was uploaded in September 2009.
      Draft reflection prompts

      • Where does a comparison clarify a system, and where does it hide a difference?
